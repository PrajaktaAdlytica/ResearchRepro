1. Scope and positioning

Recommended positioning: Research Repro is a proposed editorial evidence workbench for assessing computational artifact completeness and, after separate authorization and scoping, computational reruns. Start with openly licensed computational social science submissions and the non-executing Research Desk module.

Hero headline: Every figure deserves a traceable path.

Hero support: Map a paper’s figures to code, data, environments and permissions. Explore a proposed workflow that helps methods teams identify what is missing before an authorized computational rerun.

Required tagline: Auditable computational reruns, not a claim of independent scientific replication.

Vision: Editors can tell exactly whether an authorized result reran, why it failed and which artifact would make it reviewable.

Mission: Turn computational artifact completeness and authorized reruns into useful editorial evidence with secure execution and transparent tolerances.

Buyer: publisher methods editor, funder research-integrity lead or university research office with a batch budget and artifact access. Operator: research software engineer. Access authority: author/reviewer as appropriate to actual rights. Decision maker: human methods reviewer chooses tolerances and interprets findings.

The latest user instruction gives the master guide priority for architecture and craftsmanship, even though the guide itself normally defers to company briefs. Preserve all substantive brief content. Use the guide’s full multi-page architecture and the brief’s concept-stage truthfulness together. The current request authorizes research and IA; embedded instructions to build, publish, contact prospects or execute code are future requirements, not actions taken here.

The $100,000 ambition is a design-quality and business aspiration. It does not establish a sale price, valuation, validated demand or operating capability. The only numerical commercial proposal is the clearly labeled hypothesis of $1,000 for ten submissions; execution is separately scoped.

Current domain observation: researchrepro.com displayed a Hostinger parked-domain page on 28 October 2026. Retain the brief’s earlier RDAP 404 as historical research only. Do not repeat “unpurchased” as a current fact. Ownership and trademark clearance remain unverified.

3. Three visual territories

Recommended

The Evidence Atlas

Warm ivory, ink green, archival rules and restrained copper. Expressive serif headlines over precise interface typography. A paper figure unfolds into an evidence map. Feels like a contemporary scientific publisher paired with a carefully engineered workbench.

The Computational Observatory

Graphite, frost and amber; spacious technical sans typography; isolated layers of environment, source and output. A camera moves through computational dependencies. Stronger cinematic atmosphere, with a higher risk of feeling like a generic AI platform.

The Editorial Atelier

White, oxblood and warm grey; book-like typography, generous margins, original document photography and reviewer marginalia. A manuscript receives layered annotations. Strong human review story, with less emphasis on infrastructure.

Recommended identity foundation

Character: exact, thoughtful, accountable, calm, technically capable. Signature: one continuous evidence line connecting a paper figure, repository, commit, dataset, environment and reviewer note. A broken connection stays visibly broken until there is evidence to resolve it.

9. Pilot, forms and supporting business requirements

Pilot and demand test

Seek one paid per-batch feasibility pilot and explicit permission for ten openly shareable submissions. Start with non-executing availability/completeness audits. Compare usefulness and reviewer effort against manual checklist review and the buyer’s Code Ocean workflow; evaluate equivalent scopes and record differences. Code Ocean superiority is a hypothesis to test, not a launch claim.

Use blinded methodological staff where practical, anonymize output origin, randomize presentation order and capture baseline reviewer experience. Record artifact-unavailable rate, rubric ratings, reviewer agreement, active reviewer minutes and cost per submission. Report small-sample limits and raw denominators.
