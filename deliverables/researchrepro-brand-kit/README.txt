RESEARCH REPRO — CONTINUITY VECTOR SET
Selected direction: 01 / custom RR monogram.

20 transparent SVG masters: horizontal logos, stacked logos, wordmarks and standalone symbols in forest, pearl, mint, black and white.
All lettering is outlined vector geometry (Manrope Variable, weight 650, SIL OFL license included). No font installation, linked raster image or external dependency is required to use the SVG files.
Symbol is a geometric reconstruction of the selected concept, with mirrored RR forms, open counters and a curved continuity incision. It is not an automatic bitmap trace. The wordmark is an optically spaced production interpretation.

Favicon: simplified monogram without the fine incision. SVG, ICO and PNG at 16/32/48/64/180/192/512. 180px is the Apple touch icon. Webmanifest included.
Forest/black marks are for light backgrounds. Pearl/white/mint are for dark backgrounds. Maintain clear space of at least one vertical stroke around the mark. Use the simplified favicon at small sizes; use full symbol at 48px high or greater. Avoid mint-on-pearl.

SVGs contain editable paths. No trademark clearance is implied. Do not distort, rotate, add shadows or close the counters.
